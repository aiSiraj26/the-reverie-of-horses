The Night of Power

لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ

If the Night of Ascent carried the Prophet ﷺ upward through the seven heavens, the Night of Descent is its mirror: the night the heavens came down to earth. Laylat al-Qadr — the Night of Decree, the Night of Power — is the most sacred night of the year, when the Qur'ān began its descent and the angels fill the earth until dawn.

"Indeed, We sent it down during the Night of Decree. And what can make you know what is the Night of Decree? The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months." — Sūrat al-Qadr (97:1–3)

What follows draws together the revelation of the Qur'ān, the hidden timing of the night, the descent of the angels, the signs by which it is known, its immeasurable virtue, the worship that fills it, and the peace that crowns it until the break of dawn.

❦ ❦ ❦
The Revelation

When the Qur'ān Descended

The descent of the Qur'ān — light from the Preserved Tablet to the lowest heaven.

إِنَّا أَنزَلْنَاهُ فِي لَيْلَةِ الْقَدْرِ

"Indeed, We sent it [the Qur'ān] down during the Night of Decree." — Sūrat al-Qadr 97:1

On this blessed night, the Holy Qur'ān began its descent from the Preserved Tablet (al-Lawḥ al-Maḥfūẓ) to the lowest heaven — from where it was revealed to the Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ over twenty-three years through the Angel Jibrīl.

❦ ❦ ❦
The Timing

Seek It in the Last Ten Nights

The exact night is hidden, encouraging believers to devote themselves throughout the final third of Ramaḍān.

The Odd Nights

The Prophet ﷺ emphasised seeking it in the odd nights: the 21st, 23rd, 25th, 27th, and 29th of Ramaḍān. The 27th night is most commonly associated with Laylat al-Qadr.

Why Hidden?

Allah concealed its exact date as a mercy, so that believers would increase their worship across all ten nights, multiplying their reward and devotion.

The Descent of Angels

When Heaven Touches Earth

A mosque illuminated beneath the night sky, as the angels descend.

تَنَزَّلُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ وَالرُّوحُ فِيهَا

"The angels and the Spirit [Jibrīl] descend therein by permission of their Lord for every matter." — Sūrat al-Qadr 97:4

On this night, angels descend in great numbers, led by the Angel Jibrīl (Gabriel). They fill the earth, carrying blessings and peace, recording the worship of the believers, and saying Āmīn to their prayers until the break of dawn.

❦ ❦ ❦
The Signs

Marks of the Blessed Night

Traditions describe several signs by which the night may be recognised, though its true blessing lies in sincere worship.

A Serene Sunrise

The sun rises the next morning without dazzling rays, appearing like a smooth, radiant disc of soft light.

Tranquil Weather

The night is calm and pleasant, neither too hot nor too cold, with a gentle peacefulness in the air.

Gentle Atmosphere

Many narrations describe a serene, still quality to the night, with a sense of profound calm.

Shooting Stars Cease

Some traditions mention that no shooting stars are cast on this night.

The still, luminous sky of the blessed night.

The Virtue

Better Than a Thousand Months

لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ خَيْرٌ مِّنْ أَلْفِ شَهْرٍ

"The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months." — Sūrat al-Qadr 97:3

Laylat al-Qadr, rendered in calligraphy.

Worship on this single night carries the reward of over eighty-three years of devotion. A lifetime of blessing condensed into hours — an immeasurable gift of divine mercy for those who seek it.

The Worship

How to Spend the Night

A lantern's glow — the long vigil of the night.

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي

"O Allah, You are Most Forgiving, and You love forgiveness, so forgive me."

This was the beloved supplication taught by the Prophet ﷺ to ʿĀʾishah RA for this night — the dua to repeat above all others when seeking Laylat al-Qadr.

Recite the Qur'ān

Engage deeply with the words of Allah, reflecting on their meaning and beauty.

Make Duʿāʾ

Pour out your heart in supplication — above all the dua of forgiveness the Prophet ﷺ taught for this night.

Pray Qiyām

Stand in the night prayer, seeking nearness to Allah through extended devotion.

Seek Forgiveness

Make sincere istighfār and tawbah, returning to Allah with a repentant heart.

❦ ❦ ❦
The Dome of the Rock at night — peace until the dawn.

Peace Until the Dawn

سَلَامٌ هِيَ حَتَّىٰ مَطْلَعِ الْفَجْرِ

"Peace it is until the emergence of dawn." — Sūrat al-Qadr 97:5

From sunset to sunrise, this night is pure tranquillity and divine peace — a sanctuary of serenity where every moment is filled with mercy, and the believer finds rest in the nearness of their Creator.
