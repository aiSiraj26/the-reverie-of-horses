The Night of Power

لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ

If the Night of Ascent carried the Prophet ﷺ upward through the seven heavens, the Night of Descent is its mirror: the night the heavens drew near to earth. Laylat al-Qadr — the Night of Power, the Night of Decree — is the holiest night of the year, when the Qur'ān began its descent, the angels fill the earth, and divine mercy flows freely until the break of dawn.

A mosque beneath the night sky — the descent into stillness.

What follows draws together the revelation of the Qur'ān, the descent of the angels, the words of the Prophet ﷺ, and the reflections of the great mystics — al-Ghazālī, Ibn ʿArabī, and the Sufi saints — on the meaning of the most sacred of nights.

❦ ❦ ❦
Revelation

Sūrat al-Qadr

The open Qur'ān — revealed on the blessed night.

إِنَّا أَنزَلْنَاهُ فِي لَيْلَةِ الْقَدْرِ
وَمَا أَدْرَاكَ مَا لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ
لَيْلَةُ الْقَدْرِ خَيْرٌ مِّنْ أَلْفِ شَهْرٍ
تَنَزَّلُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ وَالرُّوحُ فِيهَا بِإِذْنِ رَبِّهِم مِّن كُلِّ أَمْرٍ
سَلَامٌ هِيَ حَتَّىٰ مَطْلَعِ الْفَجْرِ

"Indeed, We sent the Qur'ān down during the Night of Decree. And what can make you know what is the Night of Decree? The Night of Decree is better than a thousand months. The angels and the Spirit descend therein by permission of their Lord for every matter. Peace it is until the emergence of dawn."

Qur'ān 97:1–5

"Indeed, We sent it down during a blessed night. Indeed, We were to warn [mankind]. On that night is made distinct every precise matter —" — Sūrat al-Dukhān (44:3–4)
"The month of Ramaḍān [is that] in which was revealed the Qur'ān, a guidance for the people and clear proofs of guidance and criterion." — Sūrat al-Baqarah (2:185)

The Descent

Angels & the Spirit

The angels descend, led by Jibrīl, until the break of dawn.

On this night, the angels descend in multitudes, led by Jibrīl (Gabriel) ﷺ. The heavens open and divine mercy flows freely upon the earth.

  • The earth becomes so crowded with angels that, as described in the hadith, they outnumber the pebbles of the earth.
  • They descend with every decree, carrying out the commands of Allah for the coming year.
  • Their presence brings tranquillity, peace, and blessings that envelop the believers in worship.
❦ ❦ ❦
Prophetic Wisdom

The Hadith

"Whoever stands (in prayer) in Laylat al-Qadr out of faith and seeking reward, his previous sins will be forgiven." — Saḥīḥ al-Bukhārī
"Search for Laylat al-Qadr in the odd nights of the last ten nights of Ramaḍān." — Saḥīḥ al-Bukhārī
"ʿĀʾishah RA reported: I asked, 'O Messenger of Allah, if I know which night is Laylat al-Qadr, what should I say?' He said, 'Say: O Allah, You are Pardoning and You love pardon, so pardon me.'" — Sunan al-Tirmidhī
❦ ❦ ❦
The Proof of Islam

Imam al-Ghazālī

Iḥyāʾ ʿUlūm al-Dīn

Al-Ghazālī explains that the secret behind the hiding of Laylat al-Qadr is so that believers exert themselves in worship throughout all ten nights — just as Allah hid His pleasure within all acts of obedience, so that people would strive in all of them.

From the Iḥyāʾ. Al-Ghazālī connects the timing of Laylat al-Qadr to the spiritual states of the heart, teaching that the purity of the soul determines one's perception of this sacred night.
❦ ❦ ❦
The Greatest Master

Ibn ʿArabī

al-Futūḥāt al-Makkiyya

Ibn ʿArabī describes Laylat al-Qadr as a night when the divine reality unveils itself — when the worshipper may witness the descent of the angels and experience the secrets of the unseen.

From al-Futūḥāt. Ibn ʿArabī expounds on how the Night of Power represents the meeting point between the eternal and the temporal, where divine knowledge descends into the heart of the prepared believer.

Voices of the Mystics

Sufi Saints

Rūmī

Jalāl ad-Dīn Muḥammad Rūmī

"The night of power is the night of the heart's awakening, when the veils between the lover and the Beloved grow thin."

Rābiʿa al-ʿAdawiyya

The Saint of Basra

"In the stillness of this night, the heart that seeks only Allah finds its truest rest and deepest longing fulfilled."

ʿAbd al-Qādir al-Jīlānī

The Supreme Helper

"On this night, the gates of divine proximity open wide for those who turn to Allah with sincerity and devotion."

❦ ❦ ❦
Inner Peace

Spiritual Relief

Prayer and reflection — the unburdening of the soul.

Laylat al-Qadr offers a profound opportunity for spiritual renewal and relief from the burdens of the soul.

  • A time for sincere repentance, where the slate of past wrongs can be wiped clean.
  • An invitation to release anxiety and find serenity in submission to the Divine.
  • The promise of a fresh start, with hearts unburdened and spirits lifted.
"O Allah, You are Most Forgiving, and You love forgiveness, so forgive me." — اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي
❦ ❦ ❦

Peace Until the Dawn

سَلَامٌ هِيَ حَتَّىٰ مَطْلَعِ الْفَجْرِ

"Peace it is until the emergence of dawn." — Sūrat al-Qadr 97:5

From sunset to sunrise, this night is pure tranquillity and divine peace. May your nights be filled with blessings and your prayers be answered.
